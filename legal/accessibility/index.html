Home / Accessibility

Everyone gets a seat at the table.

VXVO wants its store to work with a keyboard, screen reader, zoom and reduced-motion preferences. This statement covers the current static preview.

Last updated 26 August 2026

What is built in

  • Semantic landmarks, headings and descriptive page titles.
  • A skip link and keyboard-operable navigation.
  • Visible browser focus styles and links that do not rely on colour alone.
  • Responsive layouts that reflow for smaller screens and zoom.
  • Reduced-motion support for people who request it.
  • Plain-English descriptions and honest status notices.

Known limitations

The full commerce journey does not exist yet, so basket, checkout, payment, order confirmation, tracking and account accessibility cannot yet be tested. Decorative game-table shapes are described as a group rather than individually because they do not convey product information.

Before sales begin

The complete browse-to-refund journey will be tested with automated checks and manual keyboard scenarios. Product imagery will need meaningful alternative text, and any known limits in third-party checkout or payment surfaces will be recorded here.

Tell us what is difficult

If you cannot access information or need it in another format, email daniel@vxvo.co.uk. Please say which page or task caused trouble and what device or assistive technology you were using, if you are comfortable doing so.

Current scope

Preview pages only.

This statement will be revised after the final commerce platform and customer journey are selected and tested.
