The promise before the parcels.

VXVO is not accepting orders yet. This page records the intended customer standard and the details that must be fixed before checkout opens.

Last updated 26 August 2026

Current position

There are no live products, delivery charges or customer orders. No one can currently form a sales contract on this site. Delivery destinations, services, prices, dispatch cut-offs and the non-residential returns address will be published before checkout is enabled.

Delivery standard for launch

  • Show the available method, total delivery charge and expected window before payment.
  • Accept an order only for stock that has been received or reliably confirmed and recorded.
  • Send a durable order confirmation and a dispatch update.
  • Deliver within the period agreed with the customer and, unless otherwise agreed, within 30 days.
  • Provide a clear support route for late, lost, damaged or incorrect parcels.

Changing your mind

For most online purchases of goods, a customer may notify the seller that they wish to cancel within 14 days after receiving the goods, then normally has a further 14 days to return them. The live policy will explain the return method, costs and any lawful exceptions before purchase.

Where required, eligible refunds will include the standard outbound delivery cost and will be processed within the applicable statutory period. Customers may be responsible for return postage where that is made clear before purchase. Handling beyond what is necessary to inspect the goods may reduce a refund where the law allows.

Faulty, damaged or incorrect goods

Nothing in the eventual policy will limit statutory rights. Reports of faulty, damaged, misdescribed or incorrect goods will be assessed promptly and an appropriate remedy offered. Suspected safety or batch issues will be quarantined and escalated.

Source and contact

The launch implementation will be checked against the UK Government's current online and distance selling guidance. Questions can be sent to daniel@vxvo.co.uk.
