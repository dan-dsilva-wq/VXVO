Privacy, in plain English.

This notice describes the limited information used by the current pre-launch website. It will be updated before customer accounts, checkout, marketing or analytics are enabled.

Last updated 26 August 2026

Who is responsible?

The data controller is Daniel Benjamin D'silva trading as VXVO, a UK sole trader. Contact daniel@vxvo.co.uk with a privacy request or question.

What the preview uses

This website does not have checkout, customer accounts, contact forms, marketing sign-up or non-essential analytics. If you email VXVO, we receive the name and email address you use, your message and any information you choose to include.

The hosting provider may process technical request and security data such as IP address, browser or device information, requested URL, time and diagnostic events to deliver and protect the site.

Why and how information is used

  • To respond to an enquiry or take steps you request before a possible contract.
  • To pursue legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving the website and business.
  • To meet a legal obligation or establish, exercise or defend legal claims where applicable.

VXVO does not sell personal information and does not use it for automated decisions or profiling.

Providers, retention and transfers

Vercel hosts the website and Zoho provides business email. Those providers may use approved international-transfer safeguards where information is processed outside the UK. Supplier or general enquiry email is normally reviewed for deletion 24 months after the last meaningful contact, unless it becomes part of a contract, accounting record, dispute or another record that must reasonably be kept longer. Provider security logs follow the relevant provider's operational retention.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or erase information, restrict or object to processing, and receive portable data. You may withdraw consent where consent is the basis used. Email VXVO to exercise a right; identity may need to be confirmed.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Cookies and future changes

The current site does not set non-essential advertising or analytics cookies. Essential infrastructure may use technical mechanisms needed for security and delivery. A consent mechanism and updated notice will appear before any optional tracking is introduced. The live-commerce notice will name payment, delivery and commerce providers and explain order-record retention before checkout opens.

Current data footprint

Deliberately small.

  • No customer accounts
  • No contact form
  • No marketing list
  • No advertising trackers
