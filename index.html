    Nº 02The studio

    One person who
    ships like a team.

    VXVO takes an idea from a blank repo to production — design, frontend, backend and AI — without the handoffs that slow everything down. Small surfaces, deep care, and a model layer that only shows up where it genuinely earns its keep.

    1. Principle 01

      Ship, then refine.

      Real software in front of real users beats a perfect plan. Production from week one.

    2. Principle 02

      One mind, whole stack.

      Design, frontend, backend and AI decided by the same person — nothing lost in handoff.

    3. Principle 03

      AI where it earns it.

      Models do the work they're genuinely good at. Everything else is solid, boring engineering.

    4. Principle 04

      Small surface, deep care.

      Few screens, considered to the pixel. Restraint is a feature.

    What you can commission

    • Product & interface — first flow to final pixel: design systems, prototypes, polish.
    • Full-stack web — Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, Vercel. Auth, data, RLS — production-ready from the start.
    • AI & ML — OpenAI and Claude integration, local Ollama, vision, calibration, agents.
    • Desktop & mobile — Electron and Capacitor/PWA on iOS and Android. One codebase, native where it counts.

    Instruments on the bench — Next.js · TypeScript · React · Tailwind · Supabase · Postgres · pgvector · SQLite · OpenAI · Claude · Deepgram · Ollama · Electron · Capacitor · Vercel · Python · Playwright · Vitest
