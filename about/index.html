Home / Why VXVO

A shop for choosing well.

Board games are brilliant. Shopping for them can feel like sitting an exam. VXVO is here to make the first part feel more like the second.

The idea

Start with people, not mechanics.

Most customers do not arrive thinking, “I need a medium-weight tableau builder.” They are planning a date night, buying for a family, hosting friends or trying to fill a spare half-hour. That is where VXVO starts.

We are building a compact UK range organised around those moments. Each recommendation will explain the experience in plain English, show the facts that matter and say who should skip it.

The range rule

Every box needs a job.

A smaller range only works if every title earns its place. Before a game is listed, VXVO intends to verify the exact edition, player count, play time, age guidance, language dependence, dimensions, stock status and realistic delivered economics.

  • No invented reviews, stock levels or launch dates.
  • No “in stock” badge before inventory is confirmed.
  • No opaque recommendation just because a product is fashionable.
  • No pressure to buy the biggest box on the shelf.

The person

Independent and owner-led.

VXVO is run independently by Daniel D'silva in the UK. The business is in its trade-launch phase: building supplier relationships, validating its range and preparing the operational details needed to sell responsibly.

If you have a question or simply want to talk games, email daniel@vxvo.co.uk.

What VXVO should feel like

A knowledgeable friend, minus the lecture.

  • Warm, useful explanations
  • Enough choice, not endless choice
  • Transparent launch and stock status
  • Fast, human support
  • Respect for customers' time and money
